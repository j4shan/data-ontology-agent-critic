# Greenbasket Markets

Catalog: `retail-grocery`

These questions are analytical decisions for ontology assessment. Each asks for a comparison, concentration, gap, trend, or exception across stores, categories, items, or vendors, and each one is answered by aggregating the named datasets.

## Category merchant

The category merchant decides which categories keep a promotion, a price, and a place in the assortment once sales, discounts, shrink, returns, and empty shelves are read together. The decision is which categories, brands, and subcategories diverge from the rest of their department, and which stores make a category look healthier than the items in it really are.

| request_desc | impact | question | core_datasets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Compare each category's selling rate before a promotion with its selling rate after the promotion ends. | Renewing a promotion for a category that does not hold its sales spends margin the category will not earn back. | Which categories sell a smaller share of their quantity after a promotion ends than they sold before it began, compared with categories in the same department that were not promoted? | Category; Merchandise department; Item; Promotion; POS line; Calendar day |
| Measure which categories give up the largest share of sales to line discounts and shrink together. | A category treated as healthy on the shelf price will keep receiving offers after discounts and shrink have already consumed the sale. | In which categories do line discounts and shrink events together account for a larger share of sales than in the other categories of the same department? | Category; Merchandise department; Item; POS line; POS discount; Shrink event |

## Store operations lead

The store operations lead decides which stores need a coverage change, a waste review, or an in-stock recovery when empty shelves, discarded product, thin shifts, till problems, and complaints pile up together. The decision is which open stores are the exception in their district or region, and whether the same days carry both the loss and the missed coverage.

## Replenishment analyst

The replenishment analyst decides which distribution centers and vendors are missing store demand, and whether the miss sits in the vendor fill, the shipment, the store receipt, or the on-hand balance. The decision is which centers, sites, and items show a widening gap between what was forecast, what was requested, what shipped, and what the store received.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Find open or partial purchase orders sitting at centers whose stores already have weak service. | A center waiting on an open order cannot recover a store that is already out, and a closed order is a different problem. | Which distribution centers still have open or partial purchase orders for items whose store service level is already weak? | Distribution center; Purchase order; Purchase order line; Item; Store; Service level |
