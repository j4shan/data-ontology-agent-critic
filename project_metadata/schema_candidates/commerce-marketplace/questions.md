# Meridian Marketplace

Catalog: `commerce-marketplace`

These questions are analytical decisions for ontology assessment. Each asks for a comparison, concentration, gap, trend, or exception across a population of sellers, buyers, listings, orders, or the commercial documents that follow them.

## Seller economics manager

The seller economics manager decides which sellers are commercially sound: whether settlement proceeds, commissions, fees, refunds, and chargebacks move with the value of that seller's orders, and whether the seller tier still fits. Answering requires choosing the seller population and the separate money populations that explain it, rather than replaying one fixed operational query, because a settlement line, a fee assessment, a refund, and a chargeback are different documents and no single payout shows which sellers diverge.

| request_desc | impact | question | core_datasets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Compare each seller's settlement proceeds with the order value those settlements cite, after commissions and fees. | Misreading the divergence overpays or underpays sellers and leaves a fee tier in place that does not match their order economics. | Which sellers have settlement proceeds that diverge from the value of the order lines cited on those settlements, once commissions and fee assessments in the same currency are included? | seller; order line; seller settlement; settlement line; commission; fee assessment; currency |
| Measure which sellers and seller tiers concentrate refunds and chargebacks against order value. | A seller whose reversals consume an outsized share of order value erodes marketplace margin and can require a different seller tier. | Which sellers and seller tiers concentrate refunds and chargebacks as a share of order value, relative to sellers with similar order volume in the same currency? | seller; seller tier; order; order line; refund; chargeback; payment capture; currency |
| Find fee assessments that never attach to a seller settlement. | Fees left off settlement overstate what the seller is owed and understate what the marketplace has charged. | Which sellers show a gap where fee assessments are not attached to any seller settlement, and how large is that amount relative to the fee assessments that do sit on a settlement? | seller; fee assessment; fee schedule; seller settlement; seller tier; currency |
| Track sellers whose refund and chargeback share of order value rises across the calendar days of their orders. | A widening reversal trend means the current fee schedule no longer covers the cost of keeping that seller. | Across the calendar days of orders, which sellers show a rising share of order value reversed by refunds or by chargebacks? | seller; order; calendar day; refund; chargeback; payment capture; currency |
| Surface sellers whose payout share of order value diverges from other sellers on the same tier. | Treating these sellers like their tier peers misstates contribution and delays a fee or payout review. | Which sellers on the same tier are exceptions because the share of order value paid out, once commissions, fee assessments, refunds, and chargebacks are counted, diverges from the other sellers on that tier? | seller; seller tier; order; order line; payout; seller settlement; commission; fee assessment; refund; chargeback; currency |

## Trust and safety analyst

The trust and safety analyst decides where fraud decisions and listing suppressions concentrate, and whether the same sellers, listings, or checkouts drive both. Answering requires choosing those populations and the paths that reach a seller, rather than replaying one fixed operational query, because a fraud decision may cite an order, a checkout, a fraud rule, or none of them, and a suppression cites a listing and a reason code.

| request_desc | impact | question | core_datasets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rank sellers by how heavily fraud decisions fall on their orders relative to order volume. | Review capacity spent on a diffuse fraud rate misses the sellers who account for the concentration. | Which sellers concentrate fraud decisions on their orders relative to the orders they receive, and which fraud rules account for that concentration? | seller; order; fraud decision; fraud rule |
| Find listings and sellers where suppressions cluster, by reason code. | Suppressing the wrong listings leaves risky offers for sale and hides a seller-level pattern. | Which listings and sellers concentrate listing suppressions relative to the listings on their storefronts, and which reason codes account for that concentration? | listing; listing suppression; reason code; storefront; seller |
| Measure how fraud decisions concentrate on checkouts, including decisions that cite no fraud rule. | Checkout fraud with no cited rule is the population where the rule set has the least coverage. | Which buyer accounts concentrate fraud decisions on checkouts relative to checkout volume, and what share of checkout fraud decisions cite no fraud rule? | checkout; buyer account; fraud decision; fraud rule |
| Track sellers whose share of suppressed listings rises across calendar days. | A rising suppression share is the early signal to review a seller before the same listings keep selling. | Across calendar days, which sellers show a rising share of storefront listings under suppression, and which reason codes drive that rise? | seller; storefront; listing; listing suppression; reason code; calendar day |
| Surface sellers where listing suppressions and order fraud decisions concentrate together. | A seller who is extreme on only one of those populations draws a partial review and keeps the other exposure open. | Which sellers are exceptions where listing suppressions and fraud decisions on orders concentrate together, relative to sellers with a similar number of listings and orders? | seller; storefront; listing; listing suppression; order; fraud decision |

## Fulfillment analyst

The fulfillment analyst decides which sellers and warehouses concentrate backorders, allocations that name neither a seller warehouse nor a fulfillment center, and shipments that never complete. Answering requires choosing the order line population and the allocation, backorder, shipment, and delivery populations around it, rather than replaying one fixed operational query, because unmet demand, an allocation, and a shipment are separate documents and a warehouse is present on only some of them.

| request_desc | impact | question | core_datasets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Compare backorders with the order lines allocated to each seller warehouse. | Treating backorders as a seller-wide problem sends inventory and warehouse attention to the wrong warehouse. | Which seller warehouses concentrate backorders relative to the order lines allocated to them? | seller warehouse; order allocation; order line; backorder; variant; seller |
| Find allocations that name neither a seller warehouse nor a fulfillment center. | Allocations with no warehouse and no fulfillment center are demand the marketplace has accepted and has not sourced. | Which sellers concentrate allocations that name neither a seller warehouse nor a fulfillment center, relative to all allocations on their order lines? | seller; order; order line; order allocation; seller warehouse; fulfillment center |
| Measure allocated order lines that never appear on a shipment line. | A location with a high unshipped share holds allocated demand that never leaves on a shipment. | Which seller warehouses and fulfillment centers have a high share of order lines allocated to them that never appear on a shipment line? | seller warehouse; fulfillment center; order allocation; order line; shipment line; shipment; seller |
| Locate shipments that have no delivery confirmation and sellers that also concentrate service breaches. | An incomplete shipment that also breaches service is the population most likely to become a refund or a support case. | Which sellers and fulfillment centers concentrate shipments that have no delivery confirmation, and which of those sellers also concentrate service breaches? | seller; fulfillment center; seller warehouse; shipment; delivery; service breach; order |
| Track the rise in backordered or allocated-but-unshipped order lines across order dates. | A rising unfilled share means the sourcing problem is spreading, so a one-day warehouse check will miss it. | Across the calendar days of orders, which sellers show a rising share of order lines that are on backorder, or that are allocated to a warehouse or fulfillment center and have no shipment line? | seller; order; calendar day; order line; backorder; order allocation; shipment line; seller warehouse; fulfillment center |

## Pricing analyst

The pricing analyst decides where a contract price and the seller's published price list diverge for the same variant, buyer account, and seller. Answering requires choosing the contract population and the price list population, and bringing in quotes, price changes, or orders only when the comparison needs them, rather than replaying one fixed operational query, because the two prices sit on different agreements and one order cannot show how widely they diverge.

| request_desc | impact | question | core_datasets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Compare contract prices with the same seller's price list for the same variant, buyer, and currency. | Selling the wrong of the two prices either gives away contracted margin or overcharges a negotiated account. | Which variants have a contract price that diverges from the same seller's price list price for the same buyer account and the same currency? | variant; contract; contract price; price list; buyer account; seller; currency |
| Find the sellers and buyer accounts that concentrate variants whose contract price diverges from the price list. | Negotiation effort should go to the accounts where the divergent variants concentrate. | Which sellers and buyer accounts concentrate variants whose contract price diverges from the seller's price list price in the same currency? | seller; buyer account; variant; contract; contract price; price list; currency |
| Track variants whose price list keeps changing while the contract price remains divergent. | Repeated list changes that leave the contract price divergent mean the published price and the negotiated price are drifting. | Across calendar days, which variants accumulate price changes on a seller price list while the contract price for that variant, seller, and currency diverges from the price list? | variant; price change; price list; calendar day; contract; contract price; seller; currency |
| Compare quote prices with the contract price and the price list for the same buyer, seller, and variant. | A quote that matches neither the contract price nor the price list trains the buyer on a third price the marketplace cannot explain later. | Which variants are offered on a quote at a price that diverges from both the contract price and the price list price for the same buyer account, seller, and currency? | variant; quote; quote line; buyer account; seller; contract; contract price; price list; currency |
| Surface contracted variants that have no price list price in the contract currency and still appear on orders that cite the contract. | Those variants have a negotiated price and no published list price, so an order that cites the contract is priced with no list reference. | Which contracted variants are exceptions because the seller has no price list price in that currency, and which of those variants still appear on orders that cite the contract? | variant; contract; contract price; price list; seller; buyer account; currency; order; order line |

## Returns analyst

The returns analyst decides which sellers and reason codes concentrate returns and inspections that reject the goods, relative to the orders those sellers receive. Answering requires choosing the order population, the return population, and the inspection population, rather than replaying one fixed operational query, because the reason and the return policy sit on the request, the inspection result sits on the return line, and neither document is the order.

| request_desc | impact | question | core_datasets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rank sellers and reason codes by return rate relative to orders. | A seller-wide return rate hides a reason code that is driving the loss, and a reason code hides the seller who owns it. | Which sellers and reason codes have the highest rate of return requests relative to orders? | seller; order; return request; reason code; return line; order line |
| Measure where inspections that reject the goods concentrate, relative to returns and to orders. | Rejected inspections are the units the marketplace cannot put back, so they drive refund cost and inventory loss. | Which sellers and reason codes concentrate inspections that reject the returned goods, relative to return lines and to orders? | seller; reason code; return inspection; return line; return request; order; order line |
| Track reason codes whose share of returns relative to orders rises across order dates. | A rising reason code is a product or seller problem that a single return request will not show. | Across the calendar days of orders, which reason codes account for a rising share of returns relative to orders, and which sellers drive that rise? | reason code; return request; return line; order; calendar day; seller |
| Find return requests that cite no return policy and sellers where rejections also concentrate. | A return accepted with no policy is an exposure the seller's published policy does not cover. | Which sellers show a gap where return requests cite no return policy, and do those sellers also concentrate inspections that reject the goods relative to their orders? | seller; return request; return policy; return inspection; return line; order |
| Locate fulfillment centers where rejecting inspections concentrate relative to seller order volume. | Inspection failure clustered at a center is a handling problem; the same rate spread across centers is a seller problem. | Which fulfillment centers concentrate inspections that reject the returned goods relative to the sellers' orders, and what share of those rejecting inspections name no fulfillment center? | fulfillment center; return inspection; return line; return request; seller; order |

## Marketplace finance analyst

The marketplace finance analyst decides whether payouts, commissions, and captured payments reconcile to the order lines on seller settlements. Answering requires choosing the capture population, the commission population, the settlement population, and the payout population, rather than replaying one fixed operational query, because a capture sits on an order, a commission sits on an order line, a settlement line may omit the order line, and a payout sits on the settlement.

| request_desc | impact | question | core_datasets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Compare captured payments with the order lines included on seller settlements. | Captures that never reach a settled order line are buyer funds the seller settlement does not explain. | Which sellers show a gap, in the same currency, between captured payments and the order lines included on their settlement lines? | seller; payment capture; order; order line; settlement line; seller settlement; currency |
| Find settled order lines that carry no commission. | Order lines settled with no commission understate marketplace take and overstate seller proceeds. | Which sellers concentrate settled order lines that carry no commission, relative to settled order lines that do? | seller; order line; settlement line; commission; fee schedule; seller settlement |
| Measure settlement lines that cite no order line, by seller and fee schedule. | Unmatched settlement lines are proceeds or fees with no order to support them. | What share of settlement lines cite no order line, and which sellers and fee schedules concentrate that unmatched share? | settlement line; seller settlement; seller; order line; fee schedule; fee assessment |
| Compare payouts with the settlement lines on their seller settlements, including settlements that have no payout. | A payout that diverges from its settlement, or a settlement that is never paid, is seller cash the marketplace cannot close. | Which sellers show payouts that diverge, in the same currency, from the settlement lines of the seller settlement the payout belongs to, and which sellers concentrate settlements that have no payout? | payout; seller; seller settlement; settlement line; currency |
| Track the gap between captured payments, after refunds and chargebacks, and settled order lines across order and settlement days. | A widening gap means captured funds and settled order lines are drifting apart, so one settlement day will not show the break. | Across the calendar days of orders and of seller settlements, which sellers and currencies show a widening gap between captured payments, after refunds and chargebacks on those captures, and the order lines included on seller settlements? | calendar day; currency; seller; payment capture; refund; chargeback; order; order line; seller settlement; settlement line |
