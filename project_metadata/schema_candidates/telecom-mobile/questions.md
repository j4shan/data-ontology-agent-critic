## Network performance analyst

This role decides where to add radio capacity, correct neighbor lists, and retune mobility. Cell-counter snapshots, threshold breaches, handovers, neighbor lists, release causes, alarms, and outages meet through the cell, the site, the technology, and the day. Answering requires choosing those populations and paths rather than replaying one fixed operational query.
