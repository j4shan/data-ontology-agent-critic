# Northline Mobile

Catalog: `telecom-mobile`

These questions are analytical decisions for ontology assessment. Each asks for a comparison, concentration, gap, trend, or exception across a population.

## Network performance analyst

This role decides where to add radio capacity, correct neighbor lists, and retune mobility, and it separates a voice-session problem from a packet-session problem. It owns cell-counter snapshots, threshold breaches, alarms, outages, handovers, attaches, location updates, and the release causes recorded on voice records and on packet-data records. Missing the concentration puts capacity and field work on quiet cells while congested sites and unintended neighbors stay in service.

## Revenue assurance analyst

This role decides where mediated usage is never rated, where a rated charge never draws an allowance or never reaches an invoice line, and where a line or a draw has no rated usage behind it. Packet-data records, voice records, SMS records, and content records are separate populations. An SMS record and a TAP in record do not cite a home usage record. Missing the divergence settles the wrong balance or pays an interconnect invoice the trunks do not support.
