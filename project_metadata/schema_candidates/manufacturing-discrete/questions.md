# Alderford Pump Company

Catalog: `manufacturing-discrete`

These questions are analytical decisions for ontology assessment. Each asks for a comparison, a concentration, a gap, a trend, or an exception across a population, so the answer depends on which populations are chosen and how they meet.

## Production control manager

This role decides which plants, production lines, and items miss good quantity or concentrate scrap. The evidence can be operation confirmations, scrap events, or the master production schedule, and the cut can be the plant, the line, the work center, the work unit, the shift, or the item revision. A single production order cannot show where the miss or the scrap concentrates.

## Material planner

This role decides which items and work centers are short relative to the production schedule, once issues, receipts, balances, and load are taken together. Coverage can be read from the master schedule, the bill of materials, inventory, kanban signals, capacity load, material issues, or supplier and production receipts, and those populations meet at an item, a plant, or a work center. One balance or one issue cannot show the shortage.

## Quality engineer

This role decides which item revisions and defect codes accumulate nonconformances, and which disposition codes are applied repeatedly. The record may be the nonconformance, its dispositions, the inspection result, the characteristic, or the scrap event, and those populations meet the item revision, the plant, the characteristic, or the production order on different paths. One nonconformance and its disposition list cannot show the pattern.

## Procurement analyst

This role decides which supplier sites miss receipts and which sites drive purchase-price variance. The comparison has to choose among purchase orders, schedules, goods receipts, supplier returns, the source relationship that says a site can furnish an item, and the variance on the purchase order line. One purchase order cannot show which sites create the gap.

## Cost accountant

This role decides which item revisions absorb cost, or accumulate purchase-price variance, that departs from standard. Standard cost is carried by the item revision and the plant, absorption is carried by the production order, and purchase-price variance is carried by the purchase order line, so the departure appears only when those populations are brought together. The standard cost of one revision, read alone, cannot show it.

| Rank plants and cost centers by how much of their absorption sits far from the standard of the revisions being made. | The plants and cost centers that hold the absorption furthest from standard are the ones reviewed. | Which plants and cost centers concentrate absorption that sits furthest from the standard cost of the item revisions being made? | cost absorption; cost center; plant; production order; item revision; standard cost |

## Maintenance and reliability lead

This role decides where downtime and spare consumption concentrate across assets and work centers. Downtime is recorded on the work center and only sometimes on an asset, while maintenance work orders, labor, and spare consumption start from the asset, and a spare item may or may not be linked to that asset. One work order cannot show where the loss concentrates.
