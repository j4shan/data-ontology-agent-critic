# Alderford Pump Company

Catalog: `manufacturing-discrete`

These questions are analytical decisions for ontology assessment. Each asks for a comparison, a concentration, a gap, a trend, or an exception across a population of plants, items, suppliers, customers, or work centers, so the answer depends on which populations are chosen and how they meet.

## Production control manager

This role decides where confirmed good quantity falls short of the build plan, where scrap and labor concentrate, and which work centers carry load their people and confirmations do not support. The evidence may be confirmations, the master schedule, planned orders, labor tickets, or capacity load, and the cut can be the plant, the line, the work center, the shift, the work unit, or the item revision. A single production order cannot show where the miss concentrates.

The record may be the nonconformance, its dispositions, the inspection result, the scrap event, or the calibration, and those populations meet the revision, the plant, the lot, or the characteristic on different paths. ## Manufacturing engineer

This role decides which item revisions lack a bill, a routing, or a document, which engineering changes still touch revisions the plants are building or selling, and which operations and components pile onto the same work centers. A bill is a header for a parent revision at a plant, a routing is the process for that revision, and an engineering change authorizes the revisions it touches. One revision, read alone, cannot show where the definition is missing or where a change is still open.
