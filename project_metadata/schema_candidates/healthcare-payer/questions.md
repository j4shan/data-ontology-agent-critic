# Harbor Medicare Services

Catalog: `healthcare-payer`

These questions are analytical decisions for ontology assessment. Each asks for a comparison, concentration, gap, trend, or exception across a population.

## Medical economics analyst

This role owns how condition capture and stored risk scores change expected revenue by plan, contract, and member cohort. A claim diagnosis, the category that diagnosis maps to, a diagnosis captured for risk adjustment, an encounter submission, and the month-end risk score are different populations. Answering requires choosing those populations and the member attributes that define a cohort, rather than replaying one fixed operational query.
