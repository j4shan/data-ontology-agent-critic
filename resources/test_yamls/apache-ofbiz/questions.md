# Apache OFBiz operator questions

Catalog: `apache-ofbiz`

These questions use qualified dataset names and column names only. Each one is a check an operator would run across orders, inventory, accounting, manufacturing, or shipments.

1. Which sales orders are still open with money unbilled? On `apache-ofbiz.order.order_header`, compare `order_type_id`, `status_id`, `order_date`, `remaining_sub_total`, and `grand_total`, and list orders whose `remaining_sub_total` is still above zero.

2. Which order lines are short of a reservation? Join `apache-ofbiz.order.order_item` (`order_id`, `order_item_seq_id`, `product_id`, `quantity`, `cancel_quantity`, `status_id`) to `apache-ofbiz.order.order_item_ship_grp_inv_res` (`order_id`, `order_item_seq_id`, `ship_group_seq_id`, `inventory_item_id`, `quantity`, `quantity_not_available`). Which lines have `quantity_not_available` above zero, and in which `facility_id` on `apache-ofbiz.order.order_item_ship_group`?

3. Where does on-hand inventory disagree with its detail history? For each `apache-ofbiz.product.inventory_item` (`inventory_item_id`, `product_id`, `facility_id`, `quantity_on_hand_total`, `available_to_promise_total`, `accounting_quantity_total`), compare `quantity_on_hand_total` with the sum of `quantity_on_hand_diff` on `apache-ofbiz.product.inventory_item_detail` (`inventory_item_id`, `inventory_item_detail_seq_id`, `effective_date`, `quantity_on_hand_diff`, `available_to_promise_diff`, `accounting_quantity_diff`, `order_id`, `shipment_id`, `work_effort_id`, `item_issuance_id`, `receipt_id`).

4. Which facilities hold stock that is expired or below ATP? On `apache-ofbiz.product.inventory_item`, which `product_id` and `facility_id` combinations have `expire_date` in the past or `available_to_promise_total` below zero while `quantity_on_hand_total` is still positive? Include `lot_id`, `bin_number`, `location_seq_id`, and `unit_cost`.

5. Which customer invoices are past due and unpaid? On `apache-ofbiz.accounting.invoice`, use `invoice_type_id`, `party_id`, `party_id_from`, `status_id`, `invoice_date`, `due_date`, and `paid_date`. Which invoices have a `due_date` before today and no `paid_date`? Break the open amount down with `quantity` and `amount` on `apache-ofbiz.accounting.invoice_item` (`invoice_id`, `invoice_item_seq_id`, `product_id`, `invoice_item_type_id`).

6. Which payments are not fully applied to invoices? Compare `amount` and `currency_uom_id` on `apache-ofbiz.accounting.payment` (`payment_id`, `party_id_from`, `party_id_to`, `status_id`, `effective_date`) with the sum of `amount_applied` on `apache-ofbiz.accounting.payment_application` (`payment_application_id`, `payment_id`, `invoice_id`, `invoice_item_seq_id`, `to_payment_id`).

7. Which accounting transactions that name an invoice or a shipment are still unposted? On `apache-ofbiz.accounting.acctg_trans`, filter `is_posted`, `transaction_date`, `posted_date`, `invoice_id`, `payment_id`, `shipment_id`, `receipt_id`, `work_effort_id`, and `inventory_item_id`. For the unposted ones, which `gl_account_id` values on `apache-ofbiz.accounting.acctg_trans_entry` (`acctg_trans_id`, `acctg_trans_entry_seq_id`, `debit_credit_flag`, `amount`, `organization_party_id`, `product_id`) are out of balance?

8. Which production runs are short of the quantity they were opened for? On `apache-ofbiz.workeffort.work_effort`, compare `quantity_to_produce`, `quantity_produced`, and `quantity_rejected` for rows with a `work_effort_type_id` used as a production run, and show `facility_id`, `actual_start_date`, `estimated_completion_date`, and `current_status_id`. Which of those runs also lack a finished-good row on `apache-ofbiz.workeffort.work_effort_good_standard` (`work_effort_id`, `product_id`, `work_effort_good_std_type_id`, `from_date`, `thru_date`, `estimated_quantity`)?

9. Which MRP runs proposed requirements that the last calendar said the plant could not cover? Join `apache-ofbiz.manufacturing.mrp_run_log` (`mrp_run_log_id`, `mrp_id`, `facility_id`, `run_status_id`, `proposed_requirement_count`, `failure_reason`, `started_at`, `finished_at`) to `apache-ofbiz.manufacturing.mrp_event` (`mrp_id`, `product_id`, `event_date`, `mrp_event_type_id`, `facility_id`, `quantity`, `is_late`). Which `product_id` values are late, and do they have a substitution on `apache-ofbiz.manufacturing.product_manufacturing_rule` (`rule_id`, `product_id`, `product_id_for`, `product_id_in`, `product_id_in_subst`, `quantity`, `from_date`, `thru_date`)?

10. Which outbound shipments missed the promise date, and were the units actually issued? On `apache-ofbiz.shipment.shipment`, compare `shipment_type_id`, `status_id`, `estimated_ship_date`, `origin_facility_id`, `destination_facility_id`, `primary_order_id`, and `party_id_to`. Join `apache-ofbiz.shipment.shipment_item` (`shipment_id`, `shipment_item_seq_id`, `product_id`, `quantity`) to `apache-ofbiz.shipment.item_issuance` (`item_issuance_id`, `shipment_id`, `shipment_item_seq_id`, `order_id`, `order_item_seq_id`, `inventory_item_id`, `quantity`, `issued_date_time`) and to receipts on `apache-ofbiz.shipment.shipment_receipt` (`receipt_id`, `shipment_id`, `product_id`, `quantity_accepted`, `quantity_rejected`, `datetime_received`) when the shipment is inbound. Which shipment lines have no issuance, or an issued `quantity` below the shipment item `quantity`?
